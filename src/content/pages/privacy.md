# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Disco Media collects, uses, and protects information when people use our websites, products, and services.

## Overview

We are committed to protecting privacy and being transparent about the data we collect and how we use it. This policy applies to services provided by Disco Media.

## Information we receive

We may receive information you provide to us, such as your name, email address, and messages you send through our contact or support forms.

We may also receive usage data, such as pages viewed, features used, device type, browser type, and approximate location derived from technical information.

## How we use information

We use information to operate, improve, and secure our services, respond to enquiries, support users, and maintain reliable products.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at [privacy@discomedia.co](mailto:privacy@discomedia.co).
