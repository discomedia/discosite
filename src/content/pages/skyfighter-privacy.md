# Skyfighter Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 October 2026_

Skyfighter is a game made by Disco Media Pty Ltd ("we").

**We don't collect any data.** Skyfighter has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it makes no network connections. It does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

If you choose TILT controls, the game reads your device's motion sensor to steer the aircraft. That reading stays on your device and is never stored or sent anywhere.

If you take part in testing through Apple's TestFlight, any feedback, screenshots or crash reports you send are collected and shared with us by Apple under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We use them only to fix and improve the game.

## Children

Skyfighter collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
