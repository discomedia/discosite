# Skyfighter

Skyfighter is a retro arcade shooter for iPhone, made by Disco Media. Fly through enemy formations, unlock aircraft and choose from different retro visual skins.

## Support

For help or to report a problem, email [support@discomedia.co](mailto:support@discomedia.co?subject=Skyfighter%20support).

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, the Skyfighter version or build number, and what happened. For graphics or performance problems, include the selected skin and view. A screenshot and steps to reproduce the issue help us investigate.

If you use TestFlight, you can also send feedback through the TestFlight app.

## Privacy

Skyfighter has no accounts, advertising or tracking. Read the [Skyfighter Privacy Policy](/skyfighter/privacy/).
